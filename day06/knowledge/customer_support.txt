Topic: Returns, Refunds, and Shipping Support

1. Return Window: Customers may return physical merchandise within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund.
2. Shipping Timelines: Standard domestic shipping takes 3-5 business days. International shipping takes 7-14 business days.
3. Defective Items: If an item arrives damaged, contact support within 48 hours with a photo of the package to receive an immediate free replacement.
4. Support Channels: Email us at help@acmecorp.com or call toll-free at 1-800-555-0199 (Mon-Fri, 9 AM - 6 PM EST).
