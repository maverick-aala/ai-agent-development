Company Name: Acme Corp
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Subject: Remote Work and Leave Policy

1. Remote Work Eligibility: Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
2. Paid Time Off (PTO): Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per calendar year.
3. Expense Reimbursement: Home office equipment up to $500 is eligible for reimbursement once every two years with an attached receipt.
4. Working Hours: Core collaboration hours are expected between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM local time.
